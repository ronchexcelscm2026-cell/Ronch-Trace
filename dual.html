No scanner setup is needed. Each scan goes to the right half by itself: a board not yet booked in goes to MI Line, a booked-in board to PCBA; an enclosure in the chamber to unload, otherwise to load; a driver that passed final test to Packing, otherwise to Final. Pass / Fail (Enter / Space, or the cards) always go to the half that tests. Badges go to the half waiting for one; if both are waiting, the operator clicks their own half first.
