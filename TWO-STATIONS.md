# Two stations on one computer (temporary set-up)

Used for a few days on these PCs: **MI Line + PCBA testing**, **Ageing load + unload**, **Final testing + Packing**.
The screen is split: left half = first station, right half = second. Each half has its own operator, banner and counters.
**No scanner set-up is needed** — two ordinary USB scanners are enough.

## Switching a PC to two stations
Dashboard → **Two stations on this computer** → choose the pair. The PC opens straight into the split screen from then on.

## Where each scan goes (automatic)
| Pair | Goes to the LEFT half | Goes to the RIGHT half |
|---|---|---|
| MI + PCBA | board not yet booked in | board already booked in (incl. repaired) |
| Ageing load + unload | enclosure not in the chamber | enclosure in the chamber |
| Final + Packing | driver not yet passed final | driver that passed final |

- **Badges:** go to the half waiting for a badge. If both halves wait, each operator clicks their own half, then scans.
- **Pass / Fail:** Enter = Pass, Space = Fail, then 1–9 for the reason (Esc = back), or click the buttons, or scan the PASS / FAIL / REASON cards.
  These always go to the half that tests (PCBA, Ageing unload, Final).
- Codes typed by hand are refused, as before.

## Going back to single stations
Click **Back to one station** (top right) on the PC, then pick the station as usual. Nothing else changes; all records stay.
